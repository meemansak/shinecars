How it works

The ShineCars makeover, step by step

Keep scrolling — watch your car travel through the studio while you relax at the Kafe.

STEP 01 — DROP & RELAX

Walk in, hand over the keys

Drive into our CR Park studio in South Delhi. We note your car's condition together, you pick your package — then head straight to Kafe ShineCars next door for coffee and a proper meal.

STEP 02 — FOAM & DEEP CLEAN

The signature foam bath

Thick pH-neutral foam, a careful hand wash, clay decontamination and a full interior deep-clean. Every vent, stitch and alloy is worked on by hand.

STEP 03 — CORRECT & COAT

Polish, protect, perfect

Machine polishing erases swirls and restores gloss. Then your chosen protection goes on — ceramic, graphene or PPF — cured and inspected under studio lighting.

STEP 04 — QC & DRIVE OUT

Checked twice, shines once

A final panel-by-panel quality check, tyre dressing and glass polish. Finish your coffee, collect a car that looks — and smells — brand new.
